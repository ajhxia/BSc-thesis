To Professor Spognardi, for his availability and professionalism throughout the development of this thesis. His feedback and advice have been invaluable to this work.

To my sister, my greatest source of inspiration. You taught me to have the courage to follow my own path without letting the fear of making mistakes hold me back. Even from far away, you will always be someone I look up to.

To Daniele, with whom I can be vulnerable without fear, knowing that I can show every side of myself without ever feeling judged. You are my peace in difficult times and my safe place, the person who can reassure me even when everything feels too overwhelming to face. Words cannot express how much it means to have you by my side and how much everything you do for me matters.

To my family, who have watched me grow and have always believed in me, even when I doubted myself. You have always made me feel loved and supported, and knowing that I can count on you is one of the greatest blessings in my life.

To my university friends Elisa, Bers, Comi, Jacopo, Vincenzo and Desk, who have become a second family to me in just three years. You have made this journey lighter and more carefree, turning even the most challenging days into fond memories. If I look back on my university years with a smile, much of the credit goes to you. I owe you everything.

To Aurora, whom I have known since kindergarten and with whom I have shared so much of my life. We have grown up together, going through countless changes while always finding a way to be there for each other. You are, and always will be, one of the most beautiful constants in my life.

To Valeria, with whom I have shared so many moments of my life, from laughter and heartfelt conversations to important milestones. You have become like a sister to me, someone I can always be myself around and know I can count on.

To Linda, who came into my life unexpectedly and quickly became someone special. Your cheerful personality always manages to make me smile, and being around you has taught me to approach life with a lighter heart and worry less about what others think.

To Maya and Martina, who encourage me every day to be the best version of myself and push beyond my limits. You came into my life bringing something I hadn't even realized was missing, making me feel complete.

To everyone I haven't mentioned by name, but who, even through a small gesture or a few kind words, has helped make this journey special. Each of you, in your own way, has left something with me that I will always carry.

To myself, for finding the courage to face my insecurities and the strength to keep going, even in those moments when I doubted I could make it. Today, I can finally look back and feel proud of how far I have come.
