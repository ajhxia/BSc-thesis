To Professor Spognardi,
for his availability and professionalism throughout the
development of this thesis. His advice and feedback
have been invaluable to this work.

To my parents,
whose sacrifices have allowed me to reach this milestone.
To my dad, whose ingenuity and passion I deeply admire.
To my mum, who put her own dreams aside so that I could
pursue mine. This achievement belongs to you too.

To my sister,
my greatest inspiration. You taught me to follow my own
path without being afraid of making mistakes.
Despite the distance between us, you will always be
someone I look up to.

To Daniele,
with whom I can be vulnerable without fear.
You bring me peace during difficult times and are
my safe place. Words cannot express how much
having you by my side means to me.

To my family,
who have watched me grow and have always believed in me,
even when I doubted myself.
Each of you holds a special place in my heart.

To my university friends Elisa, Bers, Comi, Jacopo,
Vincenzo and Desk,
my second family. You filled these three years with
laughter, friendship and unforgettable memories.
If I look back on university with happiness,
much of it is because of you.

To Aurora and Valeria,
the sisters life chose for me.
We grew up together, sharing changes and achievements.
As time goes by, our bond remains one of the
most beautiful constants in my life.

To Linda,
who came into my life unexpectedly and quickly
became someone special.
Your spontaneity and optimism taught me to be myself
without worrying about what others think.

To Maya and Martina,
who encourage me every day to give my best and
push beyond my limits. You were the people
missing from my life who made me feel complete.

To everyone I have not mentioned,
but who has been part of this journey.
Even the smallest gestures have left
something meaningful with me.

To myself,
for facing my insecurities and finding the strength
to keep going, even when reaching this point
seemed impossible.
